Privacy Policy for the Natural World App

Effective October 26, 2026 · Covers Matt's Natural World (Florence, KY) and Susan's Natural World (Cincinnati, OH)

The short version

  • You don't need an account to use the app.
  • We only collect what we need to run pickup orders, your rewards card and store notifications.
  • There are no payments in the app, and we don't collect your location.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Who we are

The Natural World app is the mobile app for Matt's Natural World and Susan's Natural World ("Natural World", "we", "us"). Optima Computer Systems LLC develops and publishes the app for Natural World and helps operate it. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect and why

InformationWhenWhy
Your name and phone numberWhen you send a pickup orderSo the store knows whose order it is and can reach you about it
Your order: items, quantities, store and any note you addWhen you send a pickup orderTo prepare your order and show its status in the app
Rewards card: a random card code, your stamp count, and the date, time and store of each stamp or rewardWhen you open the Rewards tab, and when staff scan your cardTo run the rewards program. The card is not tied to your name.
A notification token (a code from Apple or Google that lets us send notifications to your phone)Only if you allow notificationsTo tell you when your order is ready and, if you turn on store alerts, to send store news and sales
Basic technical information, such as your phone's operating system and app versionWhen the app connects to our servers or checks for updatesTo keep the app working and send it bug fixes

Some things stay only on your phone: your chosen store, your pickup list, your recent orders, and your name and phone number so checkout is quicker next time. Deleting the app removes them.

What we don't collect

  • Payment or card details. You pay at the register.
  • Your location. "Directions" opens your own maps app.
  • Your contacts, photos or files.

Camera (store staff only)

Store employees use the camera in the app's staff mode to scan rewards cards at the register. The camera is only used to read the card's QR code. No photos or video are saved.

How we use your information

We use it only to run the app: to prepare and track pickup orders, contact you about an order, run the rewards program, send the notifications you've allowed, and keep the app secure and working. We don't use it for advertising and we don't sell it or share it for others' marketing.

Who we share it with

Your information is seen by Natural World staff at the store you order from, and by Optima Computer Systems LLC, which maintains the app. We also use a few service providers that handle data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase, which hosts the app's database and servers.
  • Expo, with Apple and Google, which deliver notifications and app updates.
  • An email service, if we use one, to send order details to the store's own inbox.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our customers, staff or stores.

How long we keep it

Pickup orders are kept while they're needed to fill the order and handle questions about it. After that, staff remove completed orders from the system. Rewards card records are kept while the card is in use so your stamps aren't lost. Notification tokens are removed when you turn notifications off, sign out of staff mode, or when Apple or Google tell us the app was deleted.

Your choices

  • Notifications: turn them on or off any time in your phone's Settings, or for store alerts, in the app's News tab.
  • Camera: only staff mode asks for it, and you can turn it off in your phone's Settings.
  • Access or delete your information: email us at an address below and tell us your order number or phone number. We'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access limits, so only the app's servers and authorized store staff can see orders. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and only keep what we need.

Children

The app is meant for adults shopping at our stores. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For important changes, we'll also let you know in the app.
